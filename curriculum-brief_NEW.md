## Grounding formats to use

Each concept gets 3–5 of these, chosen for relevance:

- **"You'll hear this when..."** — a specific phrase someone might say, with context: who's saying it, what role Amanda is in, what situation she's in
- **"You might say this when..."** — a phrase she could use to signal fluency, in a realistic scenario
- **"Dev-to-English translation"** — what a developer means vs. what it sounds like to a non-technical ear
- **"What a non-technical person usually gets wrong"** — a misconception she can now quietly hold correctly
- **"In an interview, if asked..."** — how to deploy the concept when discussing past experience or answering with tech language
- **"So what" summary** — one sentence: why does a non-developer need to know this?
- **"Questions worth asking"** — 1–2 smart questions she could now ask in a relevant conversation
